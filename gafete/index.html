Badge

Upload your credential design and get a sheet ready to print: both sides join at the top edge, so you can cut them together and fold in half.

Paper

Image fit

Each side comes out at 4 × 5 inches (about 10 × 13 cm), a standard event-badge size that fits most badge holders. Letter and A4 both come out at real size; print at 100 %, with no "fit to page": cut the outline with the whole sheet, fold along the dashed line and clip it by the folded edge. Both sides share the same clip point.

The tool runs entirely in your browser: images never leave your device, nothing is uploaded to any server and nothing you make gets registered. It's a universal tool — 4 × 5 inches is the standard event-credential size — so it works for this hackathon or any other.

Good to know

  • This is a community-made tool, not an official Space Apps one, and completely optional: nobody needs a badge to take part or to submit a project. I built it for fun, to have something extra to wear during the hackathon; the format is universal, so anyone can use it here or at any other event.
  • The Universal Event is fully virtual, with no meetings or presentations (#1809, #1834), so a badge there is just for fun. For a Local Event, check-in and badge rules come from your Local Lead.
  • If your design uses the Space Apps logo, please follow the brand guidelines at spaceappschallenge.org/brand (#1858). And a heads-up on NASA material: the GO Team's guidance is that generated content must not use or alter NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers (#1849, #2123) — better keep those out of badge artwork too.

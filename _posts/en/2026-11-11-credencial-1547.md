I have my badge for the event. The organizers enabled a generator at [spaceappssantacruz.web.app/credential](https://spaceappssantacruz.web.app/credential): you go in, fill in your details, upload a photo and it gives you back a badge ready to print.

![My NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 badge: Alejandro Sosa, team elay puej, astronaut]({{ '/assets/img/diario/2026-11-11-6abf6897.jpg' | relative_url }})

The role they gave me is "astronaut". Below, in small print, it clarifies: participant and idea explorer. The design has a guitar-playing astronaut, so it matches the mural I'm using as my team photo while people join elay puej.

I told Siria, my wife, over chat about the challenge we're building at the hackathon: NASA data so farms can decide what to plant and when to rotate. She, who thinks faster than me at these things, answered right away: the team needs an *agrónomo* and an *ecólogo* — an agronomist and an ecologist.

I don't know where my head was, but the first thing I read was «un gnomo y un enólogo»: a gnome and a winemaker.

![Comic-style illustration of the hackathon: I'm coding on the laptop with a glass of wine beside me, a garden gnome toasts with his own glass and a smart waiter brings the bottle]({{ '/assets/img/diario/2026-11-11-7fa1dc25.jpg' | relative_url }})

I started picturing it and we'd make a lovely team. The gnome knows plants up close, he lives among them. The winemaker knows soil, weather, and waiting for things to ripen. And I type. For an agriculture challenge it even makes sense.

The bad news: the event platform won't let you register garden creatures, and finding a winemaker three days before the hackathon looks hard. So the search stays the same: if you know someone in agronomy or ecology who'd like to join [elay puej](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/elay-puej/), you can ask to join right there.

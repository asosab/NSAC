I opened a WhatsApp group, named it «Diario NSAC» and built it a listener: whatever I send there — photos, videos, links or texts like this one — gets published by itself. Texts land in this journal and images gather in the [gallery]({{ '/galeria/' | relative_url }}).

![The newly created «Diario NSAC» group, with the listener notice at the bottom]({{ '/assets/img/diario/2026-11-08-cbb89c6e.jpg' | relative_url }})

What for? Because during the hackathon I'll be more focused on development and teamwork; better that photos and a few sentences fall by themselves from a WhatsApp group onto the website. Plus — I can invite collaborators! Let the site feed itself.

This post was born from a message sent to the group, so the bridge already works.

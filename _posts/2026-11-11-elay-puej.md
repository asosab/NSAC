There's a challenge and there's a team.

The challenge is «[Field Shift](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/adapting-farms-with-nasa-data/)»: building a tool that helps farmers test crop rotations by crossing NASA Earth observations with local soil, each crop's traits and each producer's priorities. The idea is for a farm to better withstand the changing climate. SPHEREx was my favorite on paper, but this one won me over.

The team is called «[elay puej](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/find-a-team/elay-puej/)». If you're not from Santa Cruz the name may sound odd: it's a local expression, somewhere between "that's how it is, then" and "so be it". It felt like the right name for a team starting with a single person.

![Me in front of a mural of a pink astronaut playing guitar]({{ '/assets/img/diario/2026-11-11-4612a4fa.jpg' | relative_url }})

I'm on my own for now, so the team photo is borrowed: a mural of a guitar-playing astronaut I found on the street. It works until there are real people in the picture.

People are missing. I'm looking for artists, graphic designers and people who know the field from the inside: farmers, agronomy students, anyone who's set foot on a smallholding. No need to know how to code. Sign-up goes through the team page — you ask to join right there.

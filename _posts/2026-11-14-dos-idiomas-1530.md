NASA Space Apps is a global event and the site only spoke Spanish. Bad plan: people from other teams who dropped by couldn't read a word of it.

So I gave it a language layer. Starting today the site defaults to English, and Spanish moved under `/es/`, with an ES/EN switcher visible in the top bar of every page. Journal posts come in pairs: each one exists in both languages and the switcher takes you to the same post on the other side.

The old `/en/` addresses (which were live for a while) now redirect to the root. And from now on, every new text ships in both languages: if something shows up on the site, its translation goes out in the same change.

The [badge tool]({{ '/gafete/' | relative_url }}) raised two questions among participants: whether it prints properly on A4 (many people don't use Letter paper) and whether the photos get uploaded anywhere. Both answers are on the page itself now: there's an A4 paper option next to Letter, and a note explains that everything runs in the browser — images never leave your device and nothing gets registered.

Then an organizer posted the usual clarifications in the forum, and they're on the page too: it's a community-made tool, not official, completely optional — nobody needs a badge to take part or to submit. At the Universal Event, which is fully virtual, a badge is just for fun; at Local Events the Local Lead sets the rules. Plus two brand notes: the Space Apps logo follows the guidelines at spaceappschallenge.org/brand, and generated content must not use or alter NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers — better keep those out of the artwork.

I built it for fun, to have something extra to wear these two days. If it helps anyone else, here or at the next event, that's the bonus.

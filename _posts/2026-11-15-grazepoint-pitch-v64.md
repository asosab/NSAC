For the deadline we needed a short video: thirty seconds telling the problem, showing the app running and naming the NASA data. This is the cut that made it.

{% include youtube.html id="qPP_j0I15k8" title="GrazePoint: when to let the herd in" %}

The story is Sueleen's at Hacienda Patiño, who today decides by eye when the herd enters a paddock. The video shows the paddock ranking with effective re-growth days, the analysis sheet with the planting window and the PDF report that travels over WhatsApp.

This version (v6.4) already runs in production under the new name: the app is GrazePoint, the API answers at `/api/grazepoint`, and the datasets sit next to every number: NASA POWER, SMAP L4, MODIS NDVI and SoilGrids. The footage is the full tour recorded against production with the usual Playwright script; the pitch is a cut of that tour with burned-in subtitles telling the story. (This is the Spanish cut.)

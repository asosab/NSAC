Badge

Upload your credential design and get a letter-size sheet ready to print: both sides join at the top edge, so you can cut them together and fold in half.

Each side comes out at 4 × 5 inches (about 10 × 13 cm), a standard event-badge size that fits most badge holders. Print at 100 %, with no "fit to page": cut the outline with the whole sheet, fold along the dashed line and clip it by the folded edge. Both sides share the same clip point.
